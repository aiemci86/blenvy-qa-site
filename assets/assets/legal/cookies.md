# Blenvy Cookie and Browser Storage Notice

**Version 1.0 · Effective 5 September 2026**

## Scope

This notice explains storage used by the Blenvy website and application. Browser local storage and application storage can retain information even where no traditional cookie is used. The public pre-launch landing page and the signed-in application have different storage needs.

## Storage used by the application

| Storage | Purpose | How long it remains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication session | Keep you signed in and refresh your session | Until expiry, replacement, sign-out or removal through browser/device controls, depending on the session |
| Pending invitation | Continue the Group invitation you opened | Until the invitation flow completes or is cleared, or application data is removed |
| Theme and tutorial preferences | Remember your chosen appearance and dismissed guidance | Until reset, replaced or removed using browser/device controls |
| Pending optional choice | Complete a choice you made when account synchronisation becomes available | Until successfully synchronised or application data is removed |
| Application files and cache | Load the application and support browser caching | According to the browser and application update lifecycle |
| Subscription provider storage, where enabled | Retrieve purchase state and operate subscription features | According to the relevant provider and device lifecycle |

Some tutorial records include an account identifier. Clearing local storage does not delete the corresponding server Account or cancel a subscription.

## Optional tracking

Optional acquisition tracking, advertising cookies, external product-analytics SDKs and session replay are not used for the initial launch. Minimal first-party operational events may be recorded as described in the Privacy Policy. Limited error diagnostics are also described there. Any later introduction of optional tracking will be explained before it is enabled and will provide the required choice.

## Provider pages and traffic security

Hosting and traffic-security providers may use request metadata or storage to protect the website. Login and checkout pages operated by another provider may use their own cookies and storage. Review the notices and choices shown on those pages. A social link takes you to that platform; Blenvy does not use that link to enrol you in an email or research list.

## Your controls

Your browser or device settings allow you to inspect and remove stored data. Blocking necessary storage may prevent sign-in, interrupt invitations, or cause preferences to be forgotten. Signing out and deleting an Account are different actions. For server-side data requests, contact [privacy@blenvy.com](mailto:privacy@blenvy.com).
