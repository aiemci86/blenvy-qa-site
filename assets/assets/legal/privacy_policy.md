# Blenvy Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective 5 September 2026**

## 1. Introduction and scope

Blenvy is a personal-finance and shared-expense application. It helps users record personal transactions, organise financial information, share selected expenses with groups, calculate balances, and record settlement activity.

Blenvy records and coordinates financial information. It does not currently execute or initiate payments, connect automatically to bank accounts, or hold customer funds. A balance or settlement shown in Blenvy is informational, not a bank transfer or payment instruction.

This policy covers the current customer application, website, authentication, subscription, support, and related services for an initial Malta launch. It does not cover future open-banking or payment-initiation features.

## 4. Information we collect

Depending on the features used, Blenvy processes:

- account and profile information, including email address, user identifier, username, display name, avatar, authentication method, and account timestamps;
- optional onboarding answers, including age range, intended use, and how the user discovered Blenvy;
- personal transactions, including dates, amounts, currencies, descriptions, categories, source labels, transaction type, and merchant, payee, or counterparty text entered or imported by the user;
- Group information, including names, nicknames, membership, roles, weights, invitations, Shared Transactions, participant allocations, balances, settlement status and history, and a transaction link used as payment proof;
- CSV mapping choices and reusable import-template settings, but not the raw CSV file;
- support, feedback, bug reports, deletion feedback, replies, and related status and delivery metadata;
- subscription product, purchase channel, entitlement, billing state, and synchronisation information, but not payment-card numbers intentionally stored by Blenvy;
- limited authentication, service, diagnostic, security, device, browser, app-version, environment, and necessary service-lifecycle information;
- legal-document acceptance and optional marketing and research choices.

We receive information directly from you, from the authentication or purchase provider you use, from your browser or device, and from other Group members when they enter or share a record involving you. Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information and should have an appropriate reason to enter information about another person. Providing core account information is necessary to create and operate an Account; optional profile and onboarding fields are not required unless clearly stated for a feature you request.

## 6. Personal finance, CSV imports, and shared visibility

Personal transactions remain available to the Account holder unless deliberately shared with a Group or created directly in a Group. Active Members can see Group information, participant labels, roles and weights, Shared Transactions, allocations, balances, Settlements, and relevant history. Pending or rejected applicants cannot see Group financial data. Other users’ email addresses are not displayed in the ordinary customer interface.

When a user selects a CSV, Blenvy reads and parses it in application or browser memory, shows a preview, and sends prepared transaction rows for storage. The raw file, filename, rejected rows, and preview are not intentionally retained. Best-effort masking is applied to obvious financial identifiers, but unusual formats may not be detected.

Leaving or removal ends general Group access but keeps the history needed by remaining Members. On Account deletion, the authentication identity, profile, private financial data, and other private records are removed. Where deletion would corrupt another Member’s ledger history, Blenvy retains only minimum identity-stripped shared facts such as dates, amounts, currencies, allocations, and completed Settlement roles. Descriptions, notes, categories, import details, profile information, and the original identifier are removed. In a small Group, contextual re-identification may still be possible.

## 7. Authentication and local/browser storage

Blenvy supports passwordless email login and optional Google and Apple login. Its authentication provider processes identity and session information. Signed-in sessions and limited preferences are stored on the user’s browser or device. On the web this includes local storage; mobile platforms use application storage. Items may remain until logout, replacement, clearing application/browser data, or uninstall, depending on the item and platform.

Blenvy does not intentionally use advertising cookies, advertising identifiers, third-party advertising, session replay, or external product-analytics SDKs. Hosting, authentication, checkout, and other provider pages may use their own storage.

## 8. Analytics and diagnostics

Optional acquisition tracking, advertising cookies and external product-analytics SDKs are not used for the initial launch. Blenvy may retain minimal first-party operational product events to operate the service, investigate faults, protect shared records and understand basic feature use. These events use a pseudonymous account identifier and limited context such as event name, time, platform, build and screen/source metadata; they are not used to build advertising profiles. Raw operational events and daily presence records are deleted after 90 days, and user-linked records are removed earlier when Account deletion completes. Blenvy does not use automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.

We use Sentry to diagnose errors and crashes. Events may contain a redacted error message, stack trace, release, environment, timestamp and limited device or browser context. We disable session replay, performance tracing, breadcrumbs and default user identification, and apply redaction and provider-side scrubbing. We do not deliberately send financial records or account identifiers. Unexpected error content may nevertheless require investigation and removal. Sentry events are not automatically matched to Account deletion and follow the applicable provider retention cycle.

## 9. Support, feedback, marketing, and research

Support and feedback may include your message, account reference, subject, product context, replies and operational notes. Authorised staff use this information to handle your request, and email providers process delivery information. Ordinary support and feedback records are deleted 12 months after the matter closes, unless a documented legal obligation or unresolved dispute requires longer retention. Earlier erasure requests are assessed under applicable law.

Promotional emails and research invitations are deferred at launch. Following Blenvy on social media does not enrol you in either. Necessary account, security, billing and support messages are separate from promotion. Any future optional programme will explain its purposes and withdrawal route before enrolment.

## 10. Subscriptions

Blenvy Plus web purchasing is not enabled at launch. Apple and Google Play subscriptions will be handled through their respective stores when launched. Blenvy and its providers process the user identifier, product and package, purchase channel, entitlement, subscription state, period end, and synchronisation timestamps. RevenueCat may synchronise a mobile entitlement across signed-in Blenvy platforms using the same account. Stripe is not currently part of the active public Blenvy payment flow.

Blenvy does not intentionally receive or store payment-card numbers. Providers independently process billing, contact, payment-method, tax, fraud, transaction, and receipt information needed for their services.

Purchase channels are available only when enabled in the service. The checkout identifies the seller and relevant payment provider before purchase.

## 12. Retention

We retain Account and private product records while needed to provide the service and remove them through the Account-deletion process, subject to applicable legal exceptions. Minimum shared facts are retained only while needed to preserve remaining Members’ records; identity stripping does not guarantee anonymity where context identifies a person. We review that need when handling erasure requests and when the relevant Group is no longer needed.

Raw first-party operational events and daily presence records are deleted after 90 days. Ordinary support, feedback and bug-report records are deleted 12 months after the matter is marked resolved, unless a documented legal obligation or unresolved dispute requires longer retention. Legal, security and billing records are kept only for a documented obligation or necessary purpose. Provider backups, logs and diagnostic records follow verified provider retention cycles and may not be erased immediately by Account deletion. Local storage follows the lifecycle described in our browser-storage notice. You can ask us about the retention criteria applicable to a particular record.

## 13. Account deletion and export

Self-service deletion requires recent authentication and confirmation and is processed asynchronously. Pending Settlements must first be resolved. Active Apple or Google Play renewal must first show as cancelled. Completed deletion removes private Blenvy data and requests deletion of the RevenueCat customer record, but does not promise immediate removal from provider backups, delivery logs, app-store, billing, hosting, or legally retained records.

The product provides a first-party data download covering Account, profile, consent, lifecycle, legal, subscription, feature-usage, personal-finance, active-Group, analytics, feedback, support, and bug-report records available within the user’s authorisation scope. It excludes tokens, card data, raw CSV files, secrets, other Members’ email/authentication identifiers, and internal administrator notes. Provider-held data requires a manual request.

## 14. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR and applicable limitations, users may have rights to access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal, and complaint to the competent supervisory authority.

The product provides controls to edit profile and eligible transaction data, export first-party data, and request Account deletion. Restriction, objection, email-address change and provider-inclusive requests can be made by contacting [privacy@blenvy.com](mailto:privacy@blenvy.com).

We respond to rights requests without undue delay and normally within one month. Where a permitted extension is needed because of complexity or the number of requests, we explain it within that first month; the extension can be up to two further months. We may request proportionate information to verify identity. Self-service deletion conditions do not prevent a manual rights request. You may complain to Malta’s [Information and Data Protection Commissioner](https://idpc.org.mt/file-a-complaint/), or another competent supervisory authority.
